          Why "Plane"?

          Every person is a plane. Time runs along it, location runs up it, and its shape is the person's contract. A lesson or shift is a prism that pierces every plane it needs at the same time and place. Rest is a row of its own.

          You never need this picture; the Summary tab shows the same thing as a grid.

          New timetable

          New period

          A timetable in force from one day to another, such as an exam week. It starts as a copy of the normal timetable: lessons of the classes it covers are left out for you to plan, every other lesson stays where it is.

          Add absence

          Who is away and when. Then press Plan cover on the Relief card: the assistant offers a teacher for each lesson, and nothing changes until you apply a card.
